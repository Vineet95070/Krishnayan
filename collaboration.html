KrishnayanAI × Institute for Science and Spirituality (ISS)

Integrating Indian Knowledge Systems with Modern Learning Ecosystems

KrishnayanAI is honored to collaborate with the Institute for Science and Spirituality (ISS), a distinguished Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS) Research Centre recognized by the Ministry of Education, Government of India.

This partnership bridges technological innovation with India’s rich intellectual and philosophical heritage.

About the Collaboration:

Through this alliance, we deliver structured Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS) training programs that integrate traditional knowledge frameworks with technology-enabled learning systems.

What This Collaboration Offers:

  • Structured IKS Curriculum
  • Workshops & Academic Lectures
  • Guided Inquiry & Research-Oriented Learning
  • Practical Application of Indian Philosophical Frameworks

These programs are designed for students, educators, researchers, and professionals seeking intellectual depth combined with contemporary relevance.

Shared Vision:

"To enrich modern education with India’s knowledge traditions while maintaining academic rigor and structured learning ecosystems."
